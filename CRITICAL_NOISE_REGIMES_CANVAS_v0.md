# Critical Noise Regimes — Exploratory Canvas v0

**Status:** Exploratory / Non-frozen  
**Dependencies:** Structural Noise Canvas v1, Dynamic Layer v0+

---

## 1. Motivation

If noise is structural rather than statistical,
then its relevance cannot be captured by magnitude alone.

The same “amount” of noise may be:
- irrelevant,
- informative,
- or destructive,

depending on how it interacts with the underlying geometry.

---

## 2. Regime-based view of noise

We introduce the idea of **noise regimes**:

> A noise regime is defined by its qualitative effect
> on the informational geometry, not by its size.

This shifts the focus from amplitude to structure.

---

## 3. Preliminary taxonomy (tentative)

### 3.1 Inert noise
- Does not alter phase structure
- Preserves coherence and robustness
- Typical of strongly P-like regions

### 3.2 Critical noise
- Amplifies curvature or entropy gradients
- Pushes configurations toward phase boundaries
- Reveals hidden instability

### 3.3 Horizon-opening noise
- Breaks invariance
- Opens informational horizons
- Associated with NP-like black-hole regimes

This taxonomy is **descriptive**, not axiomatic.

---

## 4. Relation to perturbations

Within the Dynamic Layer:
- a perturbation may belong to different noise regimes
  depending on the metric it acts upon
- the same perturbation can be inert or critical
  depending on context

Noise regimes are therefore **relational**, not intrinsic.

---

## 5. Why this is not probabilistic

Noise regimes are not defined by:
- distributions,
- expectations,
- variances.

They are defined by:
- phase interaction,
- barrier deformation,
- invariance breaking.

---

## 6. Potential formal questions (not assumptions)

Future formalization may ask:

- Does a perturbation preserve coherence?
- Does it preserve robustness?
- Does it open horizons?

No answer is assumed here.

---

## 7. Connection to complexity

Noise regimes provide a conceptual explanation for:
- sharp phase transitions,
- instance fragility,
- unpredictability near critical thresholds.

Noise becomes a diagnostic tool,
not an obstacle.

---

## 8. Risk assessment

This canvas:
- introduces no commitments,
- adds no axioms,
- can be discarded without loss.

Its value lies in framing future questions.
