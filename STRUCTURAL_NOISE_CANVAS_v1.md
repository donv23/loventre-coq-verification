# Structural Noise in the Loventre Model — Canvas v1

**Status:** Conceptual / Pre-formal  
**Linked layers:** Robustness v1, Dynamic Layer v0+

---

## 1. Why “noise” must be redefined

In most physical and computational models, noise is treated as:
- random,
- statistical,
- distribution-based.

The Loventre model explicitly rejects this interpretation.

Here, *noise* is not a lack of information,
but a **source of structural deformation**.

---

## 2. Structural noise vs statistical noise

| Statistical Noise | Structural Noise |
|------------------|------------------|
| Random           | Configurational |
| Distribution-based | Geometry-based |
| Averaged out     | Persists across scales |
| Typically harmful | Can be informative |

Structural noise:
- does not vanish under averaging,
- interacts with curvature, entropy, and barriers,
- may *reveal* phase structure.

---

## 3. Noise as perturbation of LMetrics

Within the Dynamic Layer v0+, a perturbation represents:

> a deformation of the informational geometry,
> not a random fluctuation of values.

Key distinction:
- statistical noise perturbs **values**
- structural noise perturbs **relations**

---

## 4. Compatibility with robustness

Structural noise is not automatically destructive.

Possible regimes:
1. **Benign noise**  
   Preserves global coherence.

2. **Critical noise**  
   Pushes the system toward phase boundaries.

3. **Destructive noise**  
   Breaks invariance or opens horizons.

Robustness acts as a *filter*, not a shield.

---

## 5. Why “small perturbations” are the wrong starting point

In the Loventre model:
- “small” is not a well-defined notion a priori,
- geometry matters more than magnitude,
- a tiny change may cause a phase jump.

Therefore:
> *structural relevance precedes metric smallness.*

---

## 6. Relation to computational complexity

Structural noise explains why:
- some NP-like instances are fragile,
- others are stable despite randomness,
- phase transitions dominate asymptotics.

Noise is a *probe*, not an obstacle.

---

## 7. Design constraints for formalization

Any future formal treatment of noise must:
- preserve the frozen Dynamic Layer interface,
- avoid probabilistic axioms,
- distinguish deformation from fluctuation.

---

## 8. Role in the future paper

Structural noise will be presented as:
- a unifying concept,
- a bridge between geometry and dynamics,
- a justification for non-statistical robustness.

This canvas defines the semantic backbone.
