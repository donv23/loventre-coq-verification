## 1. Scope of Axis F

Axis F is an **experimental LAB axis** dedicated to the analysis of the
relationship between:

- classical complexity labels (e.g. NP-complete),
- instance-level difficulty,
- structural regimes emerging from the Loventre model.

Its purpose is **conceptual clarification**, not proof.

---

## 2. Core Distinction Introduced

Axis F makes explicit the following separation:

- **NP-classical**  
  (standard complexity-theoretic class label)

- **NP-instance-profile**  
  (easy / critical / hard instances within a class)

- **NP-structural-regime**  
  (P-like, P-accessible, NP-like black-hole in the Loventre model)

This distinction is **descriptive and observational only**.

---

## 3. What Axis F DOES

- Analyzes existing witness JSON files
- Produces a descriptive classification
- Highlights natural mismatches between:
  classical labels and structural behavior
- Generates **no theorems**
- Makes **no claims**

---

## 4. What Axis F DOES NOT DO

Axis F explicitly does **NOT**:

- modify the Loventre engine
- affect any policy or decision logic
- introduce new canonical definitions
- assert equivalence with classical P / NP
- claim or imply P ≠ NP

---

## 5. Relationship to the Canon

- Axis F is **NOT part of the CANON**
- Axis F does **NOT** affect any frozen canonical component
- Axis F is isolated by design

All canonical verification routines remain unchanged.

---

## 6. Integration Policy

Axis F **must not be integrated** into:
- the Python engine,
- the Coq canonical core,
- the policy bridge,

unless a future, explicit decision is taken
based on demonstrated added value.

---

## 7. Interpretation Note

Axis F should be read as a **clarifying lens**:

> NP-completeness is not, by itself,
> a structural notion of computational intractability.

No stronger interpretation is intended.
